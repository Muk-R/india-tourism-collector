### Active priority: Hyderabad pilot

Continue from the existing isolated import/API/site evidence and validated datasets. Do not repeat extraction, begin nationwide/50-city collection, discard local work, or delay the pilot for optional rating providers. The current candidate snapshot is private and is not a publication manifest. No real records become public until individually reviewed and authorized.

1. **H0 — Make the current Hyderabad slice releasable.** Resolve the public API schema compatibility migration and run it through the supported hosted build/release path only after owner approval. Review candidate identities, categories, coordinates and ODbL provenance; verify major landmarks using independent sources. Prepare an exact per-record release manifest. Keep unverified identities and entrances (including Ramoji Film City) unroutable. Add only sourced summaries with reuse terms; do not claim the candidate count as approved coverage.
2. **H1 — Curated attraction catalog.** Select meaningful attractions up to 100 per city, tiered Must Visit / Highly Recommended / Explore More or Hidden Gems. Rank by sourced historical/cultural significance, tourism importance, popularity and visitor experience; incorporate customer ratings only when licensed and verified. Keep importance separate from quality and customer ratings.
3. **H2 — Broad food coverage.** Split discovery into Must-Try Local Food and Everyday Dining. Provide multiple reliable venues per specialty across city areas where available. Cover traveler meal needs and preferences. Do not apply a fixed city-wide restaurant or dish cap; do not infer dietary or meal claims.
4. **H3 — Useful accommodation coverage.** Target at least 30 qualifying stays in each major city, with at least 10 per configurable budget tier. Increase coverage for larger cities and distribute it across tourism zones and attraction clusters. Treat these as minimums, not maximums; unknown prices, stars, ratings and amenities stay unknown.
5. **H4 — Attraction-cluster recommendations.** Relate food and stays to selected attractions, hotels, tourism clusters, transit, routes and verified nearby escapes. Use configurable geographic distances and supported road-time data; retain broad geographic coverage rather than over-serving one neighborhood.
6. **H5 — Realistic attraction-led itineraries.** Schedule only from verified hours, access, attraction-specific durations and route times. Add meal/rest breaks and multiple nearby alternatives; use selected accommodation as multi-day start/end when supported. Do not create artificial plans from missing facts.
7. **H6 — Progressive website browsing.** Preserve the approved design. Keep home previews small and in product order; provide paginated, searchable and geographically filterable category pages, with food preferences and stay-tier comparison. Keep raw intake private and separate from the curated public catalog.
8. **H7 — Optional ratings research after pilot.** Compare permitted providers for price, usage limits, attribution, caching, storage and ratings scope. Do not let this block Hyderabad; distinguish editorial recommendations from customer ratings.
