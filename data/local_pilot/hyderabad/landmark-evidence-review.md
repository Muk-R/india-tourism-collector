## Decision

- Keep all eleven individually reviewed source fixture records `draft_not_routable`; this review does not change their approval flags or fill coordinates, durations, or admission.
- The cited official pages support identity/context but are not evidence of current operations. The listed hours are historical published values, not current operational confirmation.
- Do not copy article text or imagery into Localise. Preserve source URLs and attribution; confirm reuse terms before republishing sourced prose.
- The remaining nine candidates have list-level mentions in official Hyderabad District tourism material, but lack individual identity, coordinates, hours, and access validation in this review:
